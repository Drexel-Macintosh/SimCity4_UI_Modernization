## 6B. THE MODEL'S HONEST COVERAGE — and its four blind spots

**Added 2026-07-31.** §6A tells you to ask the model before you touch the game.
That is still the right order — but a model whose limits are undocumented will
answer "nothing there" with the same confidence it answers "here it is", and
§6A's own law ("a null is not evidence until the instrument is proven able to
see") applies to `tools\uimap\` exactly as it applies to a log line. This
section is the positive control for the model itself.

`builders.json` holds **12 builders**, of which **10 carry any geometry call**.

### Blind spot 1 — the `--discover` scan never looks for `0xD8` (or for `ebp`/SIB bases)

`census.py`'s docstring (lines 16-19) promises `--discover` reports every
function that drives a window rect via `call [r+0xD4/0xD8/0xDC/0xE0]`, and the
generated `BUILDER-CENSUS.md` §3 repeats that promise verbatim
(`census.py:477`). **The scan at `census.py:308-313` does not contain a single
`0xD8` pattern**, and its ModRM list (`0x90 0x91 0x92 0x93 0x96 0x97`) omits
`0x95` (`ebp` base) and `0x94` (SIB/`esp` base).

| disp | eax | ecx | edx | ebx | SIB | ebp | esi | edi | scanned today |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0xD4` SetSize | 69 | 0 | 115 | 6 | **2** | 0 | 0 | 5 | all but SIB |
| `0xD8` SetArea(Rect*) | **66** | 0 | **73** | **6** | 0 | **1** | 0 | **1** | **none** |
| `0xDC` SetArea(l,t,r,b) | 113 | 0 | 165 | 13 | 0 | **19** | 4 | 10 | all but ebp |
| `0xE0` SetPosition | 141 | 0 | 185 | 7 | 0 | **14** | 0 | 11 | all but ebp |

- **147** confirmed `0xD8` call sites exist and none is scanned.
- **182 of 1026 sites (17.7 %)** are outside the pattern list.
- **69 functions** have `0xD8` as their *only* geometry call — invisible to
  `--discover` in principle; **86** match no scanned pattern at all.
- Including the three missing patterns takes `--discover` from **96 to 116**
  qualifying candidates (**+21 %**), the largest being `0x66DF60` (19 callers),
  `0x687960` (13), `0x7A04F0` (13), `0x9AD512` (10), `0x7EAEB0` (7).

**`sub_007A04F0` — the Data Views legend re-lay — is exactly this shape.**
Its only geometry calls are two `call [eax+0xD8]` at `0x7A082C` and `0x7A0955`;
it has 13 callers and is 1520 bytes, so it passes every *other* `--discover`
filter and is excluded solely by the missing pattern.

> EVIDENCE — `census.py:308-313` literal list; independent 6-byte scan of `.text`.
> `sub_7A04F0` disasm: `0x007A0822 lea ecx,[esp+0x90]` / `0x007A0829 push ecx` /
> `0x007A082C  ff90d8000000  call dword ptr [eax+0xd8]`; second site identically
> shaped at `0x007A094E..0x007A0955` with `[esp+0x3c]`.
>
> POSITIVE CONTROL — the null here is a **blind spot, not a fact**: the same loop
> reports 96 candidates from the patterns it *does* carry, so the loop runs. The
> zero for `0xD8` is the pattern list.

**The scan TECHNIQUE is sound — only the pattern list is wrong.** All 1026 raw
6-byte hits decode as a real `call` when the owning function is disassembled
linearly from its own start: **0 false positives**. `BUILDER-CENSUS.md` §0's
"a byte scan cannot miss a site" claim survives intact.

### Blind spot 2 — even when `0xD8` is *seen*, `constants.py` throws it away

`census.py:108` names slot `0xD8` **`"SetAreaRect"`**. `constants.py:115`
maps roles from a dict keyed **`"SetSize"` / `"SetArea"` / `"SetPosition"`**
only, so `.get("SetAreaRect", [])` returns `[]`, every argument is labelled
`a1`, and `a1 ∉ GEOM_ROLES` (`constants.py:39`) drops the record. **No `0xD8`
site can reach `constants.json` today, by construction.**

> EVIDENCE — `constants.json`: 292 sites, `via` kinds = `{TextLabel 102,
> Button 58, SetSize 55, BandArt 21, BmpArt 14, TextLabelW 14, Combo 12,
> Slider 8, SetPosition 4, CheckStrip 2, TextLabelWrap 2}` — **`SetAreaRect: 0`**.
>
> POSITIVE CONTROL — the recorder upstream is provably correct, so this null is
> a downstream **blind spot masked by a structural null**. The 10 census owners
> contain, measured, exactly **35 × `0xD4` + 8 × `0xE0` and zero `0xD8`/`0xDC`**;
> `builders.json` `vtGeom` records exactly **35 `SetSize` + 8 `SetPosition`**.
> Perfect agreement. The budget family genuinely never calls `SetArea(Rect*)` —
> so the defect is **latent, and armed**: fix blind spot 1 without fixing this
> and every newly-found site is silently discarded.

**⛔ DO NOT "fix" it by adding `SetAreaRect: ["l","t","r","b"]` to that dict.**
`0xD8` takes **one argument — a pointer to a rect**, not four coordinates. At
`0x7A082C` the pushed value is produced by `lea ecx,[esp+0x90]`, which
`argscan.classify()` reports as `enc="lea_disp32", value=0x90`. A naive role
mapping would publish **"geometry constant 144 → ×2 = 288" pointing at a stack
frame offset** — a patch site that is not a constant at all. This is the
`manufacture-a-phantom-constant` failure `argscan.py:83-92` was written to
prevent for memory displacements, re-entering through the role table.

The rect's real constants are the dword stores that precede the `lea`
(for `0x7A082C`: `mov [esp+0x98], eax` at `0x7A07FA` = `R`, and
`mov [esp+0xa4], eax` at `0x7A0813` = `B`, that offset being `+0x9C` once the
two intervening `push 1`s are accounted for). Recovering those needs a
**rect-store resolver** — follow the `lea`'s `[esp+disp]` to the four member
stores — which does not exist. Until it does, the correct behaviour is to
record a `0xD8` site as *"builder found, constants NOT recoverable"*, never to
drop it and never to guess it.

**⚠ CONTRADICTION** — `CONSTANT-MAP.md` "Known limits" #2 (generated by
`constants.py:321-323`, mirrored in
`_checkpoints\uimap-stage12.md:224`) currently reads: *"`SetArea(const Rect*)`
(`vt+0xD8`) passes a pointer; the constants that built the rect are found only
when they are register-traceable at the call."* Measured, they are found
**never** — the role filter drops the site before any tracing is attempted.
The limit understates itself as partial coverage where coverage is zero.

### Blind spot 3 — `--discover` could not have found its own census

This is the largest of the four and it is not about a byte pattern.
`census.py:301` skips any function with `callers < 2`; the docstring justifies
it as finding *"candidate primitives"*. But `BUILDER-CENSUS.md` §3 sells the
same output as *"the seeds for extending the model past the budget family
(god flyouts, mayor mode, data views)"* — i.e. as a **builder** finder. A
top-level dialog builder is called from exactly one place: its panel's init or
`DoMessage`. The filter is therefore biased precisely against the thing §3
claims to look for.

**The self-test: run the `--discover` predicate over the 12 builders the
census already holds. Eight fail.**

| builder | callers | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `0x77C660` BUDGET / City Ordinances detail dialog | 1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x77E600` BUDGET / Neighbor Deals detail dialog | 1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x786690` BUDGET / 650-wide band-set department dialog | 1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x7876B0` BUDGET / slider departments + the dispatcher | 1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x78B120` BUDGET / ordinance DESCRIPTION popup path | 1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x77A480` BAND STACKER — Ordinances F-series | 1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x77A960` BAND STACKER — 650-wide set | 1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x78BCA0` BUDGET / panel message handler | 0 | **EXCLUDED** |
| `0x77BEC0`, `0x781C90`, `0x77A6F0`, `0x77A1A0` | 4 / 3 / 2 / 2 | passes |

Attrition over the 552 geometry-driving functions: **`callers<2` removes 420
(76 %)**, `size>0x1200` removes 4, 12 are primitives, **116 pass**. Among the
420 dropped, **147 make ≥2 geometry calls, 50 make ≥4, and 80 call `0xD8`** —
e.g. `0x5C8B30` (18 geometry calls), `0x682730` (14), `0x87CA30` (14),
`0x9A1F77` (14, twelve of them `0xD8`), `0x881650` (12, ten `0xD8`).

> EVIDENCE — predicate from `census.py:300-315` re-applied to `builders.json`'s
> own keys with `funcs.json` caller counts.
>
> POSITIVE CONTROL — the 12 builders were found by **primitive call-target
> enumeration** (`by_target`, `census.py:186`), never by `--discover`. That is
> why the gap was invisible: the two halves of stage 1 never checked each other.

**The rule this earns: a discovery filter must be run against the things you
already found. If it cannot re-find them, it is not a filter, it is a lid.**
A caller-count threshold identifies *shared helpers*; a **geometry-call-count**
threshold (≥2) identifies *builders*. `--discover` needs the second, and
should report which criterion each candidate met.

### Blind spot 4 — `--discover` is the one place that skips the double-proof

`BUILDER-CENSUS.md` §0 states that *every accepted site* is double-proven —
byte scan **and** a real `call` on linear disassembly. §3's table is not.
`census.py:306-314` tests `if s in body` on a **3-byte** substring
(`FF 9x dd`) with no anchor on the displacement's high bytes, so
`call dword ptr [eax + 0x1D4]` (`FF 90 D4 01 00 00`) matches the pattern for
`[eax+0xD4]`.

**5 of the 101 candidates currently in `builders.json` → `discovered` contain
no geometry call at all:** `0x4827D0`, `0x482DC0`, `0x4832E0` and `0x915EC0`
(all matching a `call [eax+0x1D4]`) and `0x685AE0` (a `call [eax+0x1DC]`).

> EVIDENCE — per-candidate re-disassembly; the 6-byte form (`FF 9x dd 00 00 00`)
> returns 1026/1026 real calls and zero of these five.

Related, and unfixed: the `vtGeom` extractions are run through
`A.call_args()` raw (`census.py:236`), **not** `call_args_checked()`, so the
`validate()` plausibility guard that protects the 192 primitive sites does not
protect them. 43 rows, 0 flagged incomplete, but only **64 of 86 arguments
(74 %) resolved to a constant at all**, and none was plausibility-checked. An
independent sweep found no value outside `-4096..8192` today — so there is no
evidence of a desynchronised push-walk, but there is also **no tripwire** that
would show one.

### Two nulls in the model that ARE structural (quote them as such)

Per the null rule, these must never appear in an evidence column as findings:

1. **`builders.json` records zero `SetArea` / `SetAreaRect` rows.** Structural.
   The 10 census owners contain, measured, only `0xD4` and `0xE0` calls.
   `VT_NAME` *does* include both `0xD8` and `0xDC` (`census.py:108`), so the
   recorder could have seen them.
2. **`FINDINGS-generated.md` §D reports 0 edge-vs-direct divergent pairs at
   1x, 2x and 3x.** Structural — those three can never be non-zero.
   `edge_law(pos,len,f) = R(pos+len,f) − R(pos,f)` (`diff\diff.py:124`); for
   integer `f`, `R(k·f) = k·f` exactly, so the edge law is *identically* the
   direct law. Only a non-integer factor can diverge, and 1.5x does: **807
   pairs**. The tier table proves the comparator runs; it proves nothing about
   1x/2x/3x.

### What the model can and cannot answer today

**Can** — for the budget / ordinance family only: which function builds a
window; every immediate feeding x/y/w/h that arrives via a push, a nearby
register write, or one stack local, with its encoding, its `imm8`/`disp8`
ceiling and its twin set; and, for the six builders that call `sub_779660`,
a Unicorn-executed rect prediction at any factor (a prediction that depends on
`measure()` remains a HYPOTHESIS — `emu\README.md`).

**Cannot** — anything outside that family; any constant that reaches geometry
through an object field or two stack hops; **any** rect built through
`SetArea(const Rect*)`; the other 12 primitives under emulation; and
predicted-vs-live agreement, which currently joins **0 of 47** live ids
because the 50 predicted windows are budget-family while both available logs
are region-screen dumps.

**⚠ CONTRADICTION** — §6A's instrument table advertises
`diff\diff.py` as *"predicted vs live vs stock"*. Measured, only the
