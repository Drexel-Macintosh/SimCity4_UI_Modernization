**THE GOAL (northstar — re-read before any SC4 display work):** SimCity 4 runs AT the device's high native resolution (1920×1080, 2400×1600, whatever the panel is) — full world detail on screen — and the **UI elements are individually drawn LARGER** so they stay usable instead of shrinking. UI proportions should feel like 800×600/1366×768 ("built that way") at every resolution, on a myriad of devices.

**Explicit NON-goals (wrong turns already burned, 2026-07-20/21):**
- Whole-frame upscaling (render small + stretch everything) = functionally identical to just running a low resolution; delivers NOTHING. The entire dgVoodoo/DPI/ScaleRemap detour netted zero — don't repeat it.
- Any solution that shrinks the visible world to fix the UI.

**Nobody has ever shipped SC4 UI scaling — we intend to be first.**
