## FINDING 2 — the one candidate: `0xEBC619DC` at 1x (HYPOTHESIS)

    [17:54:36.173] UiSpike: DPROBE ptr0E308A14 d3 id=0xEBC619DC
                   par=0x8BCA20C3 #49 abs(495,894) 519x654 vis=1 NEW

**What.** `0xEBC619DC` is declared by `T-00000000_G-96a006b0_I-6bc61f19.ui`
— the Building Style Control script `{0,96A006B0,6BC61F19}`, the one
SCENARIOS.md AXIS 2 records CoriBoom's 36-slot mod as replacing.

**Math.** The mod's copy declares `area=(13,17,532,671)` = **519x654**. At
f=2 the law demands `1038x1308` — exactly the size REGRESSION.md records as
correct ("DPROBE proved the window was exactly 2x (1038x1308)"). Live is
`519x654`: precisely 1x, not a near miss.

**Why HYPOTHESIS.** One sighting, flagged `NEW`, no later sighting of that
id in the log. The sweep runs ~250 ms after a window is born, so a `NEW`
sample can be pre-sweep — and 13 other windows in this same log are exactly
that (finding 4). The evidence to promote or discharge it is not in this log.

**The measurement that settles it.** Aim the DPROBE band at the panel via
`[Probe]` in the ini (live-tunable, no rebuild — SCENARIOS.md), open
Building Style Control, let it settle. A second line at `1038x1308`
discharges it; still `519x654` makes it real. Per AXIS 4 this must be a
**first-open per city load** — re-opens are the case that usually looks right.

**A harness defect this exposed.** The first run judged it against *Maxis's*
script (`area=(13,1,532,401)` = 519x400) and produced a meaningless
`[-519,-146]` delta. A mod that replaces a script replaces what "stock"
MEANS for that id — the LOAD-ORDER LAW as data. `diff.py` now gives
`thirdparty-ui\` precedence for its 67 ids and *removes* the Maxis entry
rather than adding to it, so a live window can never match the wrong design.
**Standing consequence: any new third-party UI override must be extracted
into `tools\selective-safe\thirdparty-ui\` or this oracle goes stale.**

## FINDING 3 — tier generality proven offline: 824 pairs at 1.5x, zero elsewhere

Two rounding laws exist in the codebase and they are **not the same
function**:

| Law | Where | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| edge-derived | runtime sweep, `UiSpike.cpp:6478` | `SR(l+w) − SR(l)`, `SR = llround` (`:2806`) |
| direct | data generators, `build_selective_safe.py:74` | `floor(w·f + 0.5)` (same in `build_dialog_static.py:124`) |

For integer `f`, `k·(l+w) − k·l == k·w` exactly, so they are
indistinguishable. At `f=1.5` they differ by one pixel when the fractional
parts land apart. Swept across every stock size the oracles know:

| f | (id, stock size) pairs where the laws disagree |
|---|---|
| 1.0 | **0** |
| 1.5 | **824** |
| 2.0 | **0** |
| 3.0 | **0** |

The quantitative form of AXIS 1's *"1.5x is where rounding bugs hide, 2x
hides them"* — and sharper than the prose: **no amount of 2x or 3x testing
can ever surface this class**, because at integer f the two laws ARE the
same function. The 824 pairs are the exact worklist for the day a 1.5x
session happens, produced with no game and no 1.5x hardware.

`Test-UiMapDiff.ps1` asserts the 0/0/0 at 1x/2x/3x — a non-zero there means
one of the two laws changed under us.

## FINDING 4b — ONE-AXIS-EXACT: content-sized windows identify themselves

Added after the live log changed mid-run and surfaced `0x4A9DB60C`
(the message/popup box) at `900x450` where the law demanded `900x492`:

    width  900 == 2 x 450   EXACT
    height 450 vs 492       off by 42

A uniform scaling defect moves BOTH axes. It essentially never moves exactly
one and leaves the other pixel-perfect. When one axis satisfies the law to
the pixel and the other does not, the failing axis is **content or art
driven** — REGRESSION.md law 17 ("a style-PNG widget is born at the ART's
size"), law 21 ("text laid out once at creation does not re-wrap"), and the
whole `kFontSizedIds` family. `diff.py` now emits `ONE-AXIS-EXACT` with the
good axis named, so these are neither ignored nor mistaken for scaling bugs.

Confirmed on the next capture: the same window reached `900x492` at
`18:13:40`, i.e. it was mid-resize while the popup wrap work was in flight.

**Consequence for the suite baseline:** the log is REWRITTEN each launch and
grows while the user plays, so a tolerated-row COUNT is worthless — which
windows get captured varies per session. `Test-UiMapDiff.ps1` therefore
baselines **by id** (`$KNOWN`, one entry, with its reason), in the style of
`Test-DatIntegrity.ps1`'s EXPECTED table. It also prints a NOTE when the log
was written in the last two minutes, so a mid-sweep capture is not mistaken
for a regression.

## FINDING 8 — what this harness still CANNOT see

Stated plainly so it is not mistaken for a clean bill of health.

1. **No 1.5x or 3x live log exists.** Tier generality is proven only as far
   as offline math reaches. Nothing here has *observed* a 1.5x frame.
2. **The only inert oracle is a region-screen boot dump at 800x600 from
   v2.6.0.** `ScaleAll=0` proves the SWEEP was off, not that all 2x DATA was
   parked. **A founded-city inert capture is the single highest-value
   addition to this harness** — it would give measured stock rects for the
   city HUD and the budget family at once. `_tests\Set-StockCompare.ps1` is
   the existing lever; SCENARIOS.md already lists it as a TODO for an
   unrelated reason.
3. **Positions are checked only where meaningful.** A parent-relative rect
   with a real parent window is compared; a top-level window anchored to the
   screen is not (its position depends on render resolution). Five such are
   classed `SCREEN-SIZED` rather than judged.
4. **The `stock-budget` PNGs are never opened** — deliberate, per
   STOCK-REFERENCE.md's directive that every verdict be geometry and math.
   Real consequence: those ten captures cannot contribute rects, which is
   part of why finding 5's 35 unknowns exist.
5. **Generic ids (`<= 0xFF`) are excluded from every defect bucket** and
   bucketed `AMBIGUOUS-ID` (23 rows). Joining on a reused placeholder id is
   noise, not evidence.

## NOVEL WORK (METHOD.md section 3 routing / law 23)

- **The `panel` line is a before/after pair and is therefore the project's
  strongest geometry oracle.** It had never been parsed. Mechanism: the
  sweep logs `l,t,w,h` captured before `SetW/SetH` and the computed target
  after (`UiSpike.cpp:6558-6569`), so stock and scaled sit on one line at
  one resolution. *(Promote to `SC4-UI-ENGINE.md` if it outlives this.)*
- **Two rounding laws exist and diverge only at 1.5x** (finding 3). Any tool
  assuming one law is wrong at 1.5x, and no 2x/3x test can catch it.
- **The `.UI` stock oracle is blind to third-party script replacement**
  (finding 2). Fixed by precedence, with a standing maintenance obligation.
- **WHAT IT COST:** a naive id-join over these same logs reported ~3000
  defects. Four filters — on-screen gate (effective visibility, one hidden
  ancestor tolerated), ever-correct exclusion, generic-id exclusion, and the
  pre-sweep/recurring temporal split — reduce it to **one** candidate
  without discarding a single confirmed defect. Every one was transcribed
  from an existing project lesson rather than invented; three were already
  written down in `Audit-UnscaledWindows.py` and `SCENARIOS.md`. That is
  METHOD.md rule 1 paying for itself.
